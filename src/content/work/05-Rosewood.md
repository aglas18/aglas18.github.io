longDescription: >
  Rosewood Bourbon is a conceptual spirits brand developed as part of a corporate branding exploration. The project investigates how a modern bourbon brand can balance tradition and craftsmanship with a more contemporary, refined visual identity.

  The work includes logo design, packaging and label concepts, stationery, merchandise, and a promotional website. A key focus was building a strong, timeless identity system that communicates luxury, heritage, and attention to detail, establishing a cohesive foundation for the entire brand experience.

heroIntro: >
  <p>
    Rosewood Bourbon is a conceptual spirits brand that explores how a traditional category like bourbon can be reinterpreted through a modern branding lens. Rooted in ideas of craftsmanship, heritage, and refinement, the project focuses on building a visual identity that feels both timeless and contemporary. The goal was to create a cohesive brand system that could extend across packaging, print, digital, and promotional materials while maintaining a strong sense of luxury and consistency throughout.
  </p>

goalSection:
  title: Defining a Modern Heritage Brand
  body: >
    The objective of this project was to create a bourbon brand that feels rooted in tradition while still appealing to a modern audience. Many spirits brands rely heavily on heritage cues, so the challenge was to strike a balance between authenticity and contemporary refinement.

    The focus was placed on developing a strong foundational identity system, starting with the logo and extending into packaging, stationery, merchandise, and a promotional web experience. Each touchpoint was designed to reinforce the idea of craftsmanship, quality, and understated luxury.
