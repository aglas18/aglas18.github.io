longDescription: >
  Rosewood Bourbon is a conceptual spirits brand developed as part of a corporate branding exploration. The project investigates how a modern bourbon brand can balance tradition and craftsmanship with a more contemporary, refined visual identity.

  The work includes logo design, packaging and label concepts, stationery, merchandise, and a promotional website. A key focus was building a strong, timeless identity system that communicates luxury, heritage, and attention to detail, establishing a cohesive foundation for the entire brand experience.
