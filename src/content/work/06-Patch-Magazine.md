longDescription: >
  Patch Magazine is an independent publication dedicated to indie games, serving both readers and developers within a growing creative community. The homepage redesign focused on transforming a limited, action-light experience into a more intentional user journey — one that makes it easier to purchase issues and subscriptions, discover editorial content, and engage with the Patch community.

  The updated design introduces stronger visual hierarchy, multiple conversion touchpoints, and responsive layouts adapted across desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints to ensure a consistent and engaging experience regardless of device.

goalSection:
  title: Redesigning the Homepage Experience
  body: >
    The original Patch homepage provided limited opportunities for users to take action, with back issues, subscriptions, and editorial content difficult to find. The redesign focused on creating a more purposeful page structure — one that guides visitors from discovery to purchase while surfacing the publication's growing library of indie game content.

    Key priorities included introducing clearer conversion paths, improving content visibility for SEO and editorial discovery, and building a stronger community experience that reflects Patch's role beyond selling magazines.

caseSections:
  - type: mediaText
    mediaPosition: left
    eyebrow: Conversion
    title: Creating Clearer Conversion Paths
    body: >
      <p>
        One of the primary goals of the redesign was to make it easier for visitors to purchase issues and subscriptions. The original homepage provided limited opportunities for users to take action, so the updated experience introduces multiple conversion points throughout the page. Above the fold, a dedicated call-to-action encourages visitors to shop the latest issue, while a countdown banner creates urgency around upcoming releases. Subscription options were also restructured into dedicated product cards, allowing users to easily compare plans, add products directly to their cart, and purchase with fewer steps.
      </p>

      <p>
        As an independent publication focused on indie games, Patch has a growing library of content that deserves greater visibility. The redesign introduces stronger visual hierarchy through larger imagery, clearer content organization, and expanded editorial sections that help users discover more of the magazine's offerings. Additional SEO-focused content was incorporated to provide context about the publication while supporting organic search visibility. Back issues and featured content were also surfaced more prominently, creating additional entry points into the Patch ecosystem.
      </p>

To support this community-focused mission, the homepage was redesigned to highlight social proof and encourage deeper engagement. An interactive testimonial carousel showcases reader feedback alongside supporting imagery, while a tabbed content section allows visitors to explore blog content, featured games, and social channels without leaving the page. Dedicated community calls-to-action were also introduced, encouraging users to join the Patch Discord and providing opportunities for developers interested in working with the publication.

A dedicated closing section provides a final opportunity to subscribe or purchase an issue, helping reinforce key actions while creating a stronger conclusion to the browsing experience. The final design was adapted across desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints to ensure a consistent and engaging experience regardless of device.
